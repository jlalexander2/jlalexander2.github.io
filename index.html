PhishSense

PhishSense is an adaptive phishing awareness training platform designed to help users recognize suspicious emails and improve their cybersecurity decision-making.

The project combines simulated email scenarios, performance-based training adjustments, and educational feedback.

The initial application foundation includes a SQLite database, user registration, authentication, and a learner dashboard.
