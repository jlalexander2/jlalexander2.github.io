Master's Capstone Project

PhishSense

PhishSense is an adaptive phishing awareness training platform being developed as part of my Master of Science in Computer Science capstone project.

The system is designed to help users improve their ability to identify suspicious emails through simulated phishing scenarios, interactive training, and performance feedback.

The planned adaptive training component will adjust scenario difficulty based on learner performance.

The application uses Python, Flask, and SQLite. Current development includes user registration, authentication, a learner dashboard, and initial application routes.
